Virtual Apartment

A 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment (75 m²). Walk around and rearrange it.

  1. Walk in. Click to start, then use the mouse to look around and WASD to walk.
  2. Move furniture. Aim at an item and click to pick it up. Click again to put it down. A green ghost means the spot is free, and red means it's blocked.
  3. Add & style. Press B to add furniture and M to change floors, walls and colors. Small decor can go on tables and counters.
  4. Take a picture. Press P to hide the interface and click to save a photo.
  5. Don't worry. Everything saves automatically. Ctrl+Z undoes, and Esc frees the mouse and shows the menu.
